Everything You Need to Know

How does the 1.00% daily ROI work? +
Once your investment package is activated via crypto deposit, our automated XAUUSD trading algorithms generate returns. 1.00% is credited directly to your platform earnings balance daily.
When can I withdraw my earnings? +
Payout cycles run every 14 days (fortnightly). On each 14th day milestone, your accumulated daily earnings become unlocked for withdrawal to your USDT (TRC20/BEP20) or Binance Pay address.
How do I qualify for the monthly salary? +
Achieve confirmed direct referral volume milestones (starting at $1,000 for Starter Tier). Salaries are paid on the 5th of every month for up to 6 active months per qualified tier.
What payment methods are supported for deposits? +
We accept USDT TRC20, USDT BEP20, and Binance Pay direct transfers. Deposits are confirmed promptly once blockchain confirmations are received.
